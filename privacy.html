Privacy Policy

TestBot · Last updated November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information TestBot ("the Bot"), a personal Discord bot run by Zihan ("the operator"), handles and why.

1. Information the Bot handles

  • Discord identifiers: your Discord user ID, username and display name, and the IDs of the channels and servers where you use the Bot.
  • Message content: the text and attachments of messages you send to the Bot, either in direct messages or in channels the operator has enabled.
  • Access records: a list of approved user IDs and short-lived pairing codes used to approve new users.

The Bot does not read messages in channels the operator has not enabled, and it does not collect payment details, passwords, location or contact lists.

2. How it is used

  • to decide whether you are allowed to use the Bot;
  • to pass your messages to an AI assistant and send its replies back to you;
  • to keep the Bot working and prevent abuse.

Your information is never sold, rented or used for advertising.

3. Who else receives it

  • Discord carries all messages to and from the Bot. See Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic processes message content to generate the assistant's replies. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

Information is not shared with anyone else unless required by law.

4. Storage and retention

The access list is stored in a local file on the operator's computer and kept until you ask to be removed or the operator removes you. Message content may remain in the operator's local assistant session history, which the operator may delete at any time. Discord and Anthropic keep data according to their own policies.

5. Your choices

  • Stop using the Bot at any time by no longer messaging it.
  • Ask the operator to remove you from the access list and delete stored information about you.
  • Ask what information about you is stored.

6. Children

The Bot is not intended for anyone under the minimum age required by Discord or by local law.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the date above will be updated.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, send a direct message to the operator on Discord.

TestBot is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discord Inc. or Anthropic.
